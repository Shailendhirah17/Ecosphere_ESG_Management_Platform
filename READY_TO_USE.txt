📈 BUSINESS FORMULAS

Environmental Score:
  = 100 - (total_emissions / baseline * 50)

Social Score:
  = MIN(100 + (hours/10) + (impact/100), 100)

Governance Score:
  = 100 - (open_issues * 5) - (overdue_issues * 10) + (compliance_rate * 0.5)

Overall Score:
  = (Env * 40% + Social * 30% + Gov * 30%) / 100
